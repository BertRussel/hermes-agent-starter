# 10. Intake and planning

## Actor and authority
The owner defines the outcome and material permissions. The controller settles design decisions before fanning out. Workers do not independently choose shared names, schemas or architecture.

## Prerequisites and version
No autonomous launch occurs before owner-direction readiness and native admission. A mechanically valid task cannot repair an unclear product objective.

## Inputs
Outcome, sources, scope/non-goals, constraints, acceptance evidence, authority, dependencies, human gates and report destination.

## Procedure
Distinguish a question from an authorized mutation. Explain tradeoffs and a recommendation before asking a consequential choice. When clarification controls are appropriate, use the real tool and semantic unnumbered options; the renderer owns numbering and numeric buttons. Other appears once. An unanswered/expired control is not consent. Do not ask the owner to approve routine already-authorized substeps. Freeze shared decisions into every dependent child packet. Choose direct deterministic work for small coupled tasks; use independent roles only when scope and review benefit warrant them. Register the native root, exact notify+wake route and timing before any autonomous child can become runnable.

## Expected results
A complete packet contains measurable acceptance criteria and genuine narrow owner gates. Technical defaults do not become invented owner restrictions. No task starts with contradictory source or authority.

## Independent verification
Read back the literal card and dependency graph before release. Verify each fact against its source category and each claimed approval against authenticated owner direction.

## Failure and recovery
Material ambiguity parks intake for the specific missing decision. An expired picker requires safe recovery of semantic intent. Do not create a second root merely because the chat observer or notification fails.

## Privacy and outputs
Keep owner-specific sources and decisions private; ship only neutral packet templates and schemas. Status notifications are not authority.
