# 15. Research workflow

## Actor and authority
The researcher answers a bounded evidence question. It does not implement architecture, alter accounts, publish findings or make the controller's final decision.

## Prerequisites and version
An admitted research child names permitted source classes, output scope and stop condition. Browser/provider access is explicit and must not inherit live credentials.

## Inputs
Question, decision context, source ownership, retrieval constraints, confidence standard and private artifact destination.

## Procedure
Locate the smallest owning source/index first. Distinguish primary evidence, interpretation and unsupported claims. Read original sources, record citation/retrieval date and independently verify material quotes or quantitative facts with tools. Seek contradictions rather than collecting only supportive links. If genuinely independent lanes help, give bounded read-only leaves distinct questions and verify their results before integration. File durable findings under Research or the owning project; update Research Index and link source/original locations. Formal code acceptance is a separate verifier/reviewer route, not a research opinion.

## Expected results
The report contains a sourced answer, uncertainty, contradictions, unavailable evidence and implications limited to the question. No fabricated quotations, dates, statistics or inaccessible-source summaries occur.

## Independent verification
The controller opens cited sources and checks decisive claims. Confirm links resolve, evidence predates the decision and source classes match authority. Local RSS parsing tests prove parsing only, not remote factual correctness.

## Failure and recovery
A blocked fetch is evidence for that route. Try documented permitted alternatives without bypassing access restrictions; state the specific unresolved source rather than inventing content. Conflicting sources require attributable reconciliation, not a silently selected convenient version.

## Privacy and outputs
Private client research stays in the owner vault/project. Public reports need a separate sanitized exact-byte review and publication grant.
