Factory source selection/host proofs are not portable public acceptance. Proprietary creative source and unresolved timing/evidence rights cannot be distributed under a presumed MIT umbrella. Source-owned SOUL.md is overwritten by native update; do not claim preservation of a customized owner identity from a same-version test.

Code-phase additions are intentionally bounded:

- The official public acquisition utility accepts only the official upstream URL
  and a full exact commit. It scrubs authentication/config inheritance and never
  silently falls back to a private checkout. Acquisition alone certifies neither
  compatible native extensions nor redistribution rights or installation.
- Neutral component CI is exercised from an exact-file, history-free restored
  export, with no factory home. Its four portable checks do not certify a full
  public installation or the governed runtime.
- The credential-free research alternative retrieves only an explicitly allowed
  HTTPS URL (HTTP is loopback-fixture only), refuses redirects and credential URLs,
  caps bytes/time, and records source-byte hashes. A real loopback HTTP fixture
  passes; external research and JavaScript browser acceptance remain pending.
- Native canaries use new scrubbed homes and DBs. They never start a Gateway,
  dispatch roles, synthesize PASS verdicts, or claim owner approval.
